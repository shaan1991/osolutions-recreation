Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and how to ask us to delete it. Effective September 4, 2026.

This is a plain language summary of the original policy. The original notes it is a draft not reviewed by counsel and is not legal advice.

Who is responsible

The controller is OSolutions LLC, based in the United States. The mailing address was still a placeholder on the original policy at the time of writing. Questions about privacy go to privacy@knowledgeable-solutions.com. There is no dedicated data protection officer.

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, email address, and message from the contact form, plus your planner answers if you arrive from the project estimator. We also process your IP address for rate limiting and for the Turnstile anti spam check.

Why we collect it

To answer your inquiry and prepare for a possible engagement. The legal bases are legitimate interest and steps taken before a contract. We do not use your details for marketing.

How long we keep it

Leads are kept for 24 months and then deleted automatically. Encrypted backups are kept no longer than 30 days.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your data. Cloudflare acts as a processor for delivery, security, and anti spam, under the EU US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

Cookies

No analytics or tracking cookies. The site only sets what it needs to function: an admin session cookie plus the Cloudflare security cookies (__cf_bm and cf_clearance).

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, or deletion of your data at privacy@knowledgeable-solutions.com. The policy commits to a response within one month.
